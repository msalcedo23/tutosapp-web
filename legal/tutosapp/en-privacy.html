Version 1.0. Effective November 11, 2026.

This policy explains what we do with the information you send us when you tell us about your idea on tutosapp.co, through the "Tell us your idea" chat, by email or on WhatsApp. It is part of the personal data processing policy of TUTOS APP S.A.S., under Colombia’s Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1074 of 2015 (chapter 25, which includes Decree 1377 of 2013). Each app also has its own policy, linked from its page.

2. The short version

  • We use your information only to reply to you, understand your idea and, if you want, prepare a proposal and schedule a call.
  • Your idea is yours: we treat it as confidential, we do not share it or use it for anything else, and if you want, we sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before the first call.
  • We do not sell your data or send you advertising.
  • This site uses no cookies and no analytics.

3. What we use and why

  • What you write in the chat: your name, your email or WhatsApp, your company or project (if you give it), your idea, the type of project, when you want to start, the budget you have in mind, whether you want an NDA and the language of the page. We use it to reply to you, understand your project, prepare a proposal and schedule a call.
  • Your consent: that you gave it, with the date, as proof that you allowed us to write to you.
  • Technical connection data, such as your IP address: used to prevent abuse (for example, at most 5 messages per hour from one connection), and it may remain in the provider’s technical logs.
  • If you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we use what you send, for the same purposes.

We do not ask for sensitive data. Please do not include it in your message.

4. Who else processes it

The chat sends your answers to a Tutos App function hosted on Supabase, in the United States, which stores them privately and notifies us. Supabase processes the data on behalf of TUTOS APP S.A.S., as a processor, under its data processing agreement. Your information leaves Colombia for the United States, which Colombia’s Superintendence of Industry and Commerce lists among the countries with an adequate level of protection. If you email us or message us on WhatsApp, your message also goes through those services.

We do not share your idea or your data with anyone else, unless an authority requires it under the law.

5. How long we keep it

While we talk about your project and up to 2 years after the last contact, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. If we work together, what is needed is kept under the contract and as commercial law requires.

6. Your rights

As the owner of your data, you can:

  • Know, update and correct your data.
  • Ask for proof of the consent you gave us.
  • Know how we have used it.
  • Withdraw your consent and ask us to delete your data, when there is no legal or contractual duty to keep it.
  • Access your data free of charge.
  • File complaints with Colombia’s Superintendence of Industry and Commerce, after first making the request or complaint to us.

To exercise them, email hola@tutosapp.co with your name, how to reach you and what you are asking for.

Deadlines. A request is answered within 10 business days at most; if that is not possible, we tell you why and answer within the next 5 business days. A complaint is answered within 15 business days at most; if that is not possible, we tell you why and answer within the next 8 business days. If a complaint is incomplete, we tell you within 5 days so you can complete it; if 2 months pass without it being completed, it is considered withdrawn.

7. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is kept in private storage, which only the Tutos App team that handles messages can access.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we publish the new version on this page with its date. If a change affects what we use your data for, we ask for your consent again.
