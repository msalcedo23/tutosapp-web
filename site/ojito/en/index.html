What it does for you

  • An answer for everyone

    What works for you may not work for your baby. Ojito gives you your result and, below it, one for each person in your home by age.

  • Allergies and conditions

    Flag peanuts, dairy, gluten, diabetes or high blood pressure, among others, and Ojito warns you when it sees them on the label.

  • Built for Colombia

    The score looks at Colombian warning labels, additives and how processed the product is. For personal care it checks ingredients against Andean Community and European Union lists.

  • No account, no paid placement

    No sign-up, and no names or photos of your children. No brand pays to appear or to improve its score.

How it works

  1. Point at the barcode

    Ojito reads it on its own, no photo needed. You can also type the code.

  2. See your home’s result

    Good, Fair or Avoid with a score from 0 to 100 for you, plus a line for your baby or another person in your family.

  3. Understand why

    Tap "See why" to learn what drove the score, and see similar options with a better score.

Frequently asked questions

Which products does it work with?

Packaged food and personal-care products with a barcode. If a product is missing, take a photo of the nutrition table and the ingredients, and Ojito’s AI reads them.

How does it know what is good for my baby?

It only asks for their age, never a name or photos. Below your result you then see a line for your baby, for pregnancy or breastfeeding, or for another adult at home, with the reason in a few words.

Does Ojito replace a pediatrician or doctor?

No. Ojito is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. It helps you read the label. No alert does not mean the product is free of that allergen: always check the package.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and scan. Your family’s details stay on your phone.

How much does it cost?

Scanning, the result, the reasons, your family’s alerts and other options cost nothing. An optional subscription, Ojito Familia, will later add full history and a shopping list.

When does it launch?

Ojito is in testing and coming soon to Google Play and the App Store. To try it early on Android, email us and we will add you to the testing group.
