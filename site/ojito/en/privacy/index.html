This is an English translation of the Política de privacidad de Ojito. If the two versions differ, the Spanish version prevails.

Ojito is an app that checks packaged products from their barcode. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, how long it is kept, and how to exercise your rights. It is also the personal data processing policy of TUTOS APP S.A.S., under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1074 of 2015 (chapter 25, which incorporates Decree 1377 of 2013).

2. The most important points

  • There is no account and no sign-up. Ojito does not ask for your name, your email or your phone number.
  • The ages, birth dates and profiles of your family stay on your phone.
  • What the app needs to work leaves the phone: the product code, an installation identifier, the package photos and notes you decide to send and, only if you authorize it, what you type by hand about a condition.
  • If you subscribe to Ojito Familia, Google Play or the App Store charges you. Ojito does not see your card, your name or your email.
  • Ojito shows no ads and does not sell your information.

3. What stays on your phone

We do not send to our servers:

  • The age or birth date of a baby or child.
  • Your family's profiles and what you mark in the lists of things to watch, such as allergies, conditions, pregnancy or breastfeeding.
  • Your text size, appearance and voice preferences.
  • Your history of checked products and your shopping list.

This information is not included in Android or iOS backups. It is deleted when you remove the profile or uninstall the app.

The camera is used only to read barcodes and take photos of packages. Photos you choose from your gallery are used only for the same purpose. The check the app makes on a photo before sending it runs on the phone.

5. Health data, data about minors, and your authorization

What you write about a condition may be sensitive data, because it is about health. Before sending it, the app asks for your express authorization, which also covers what you write later in your family, including your children, until you withdraw it. You are not required to give it, and no feature depends on it: if you don't give it, the text stays on the phone and the app checks only what it can read there. You can also use the options in the list, which do not leave the phone.

Do not write names in that text. If it is about a child or adolescent, you authorize as their representative, and you should only do so if it is in their interest. We do not ask for children's names or photos.

For photos, you authorize by tapping the send button, right after the notice shown above it. For the other data, you authorize by continuing from the app's welcome screen. If you do not agree, do not keep using Ojito.

The app saves on your phone the version and date of your authorization for the text. You can withdraw it in Settings.

6. Who it is shared with, and outside Colombia

  • Supabase hosts the database, the photos and the server functions, in the United States.
  • Anthropic, PBC provides the artificial intelligence service that reads the photos and the text, from outside Colombia. According to its published terms, consulted on November 1, 2026, it deletes those inputs and outputs within 30 days, except in cases such as enforcing its usage policy or complying with the law. It also states that it does not train its models on this data unless the customer opts in.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) delivers emails to the Ojito team. When you tell us something is wrong or send us photos of a product, the team receives an email with the product, what is wrong if you tell us, your note and links to your photos that expire after 7 days. The photos are not inside the email. That email does not carry your identifier or anything about your family, and it stays in the team's mailboxes.
  • Google receives the ML Kit technical metrics described above.
  • Google Play and Apple's App Store charge for Ojito Familia as controllers in their own right, under their own terms and privacy policies. Ojito does not see your card, your name or your email. When you buy, the app passes them the installation identifier, so the purchase is tied to that phone. Afterwards, Ojito asks them with the purchase receipt whether it is still valid, and they answer with the plan, the status and the expiry.
  • Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts receive your IP address and the product code when your phone loads the photo of a suggested option. They are not Ojito's providers: they act on their own, under their own terms, and may be outside Colombia.
  • The people who review products for Ojito can see the photos you send.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Supabase, Anthropic and Brevo process the data on behalf of TUTOS APP S.A.S., as processors, under their data processing agreements, and must give it protection equal or equivalent to this policy. Your information leaves Colombia for countries such as the United States and France, which the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio) lists among those with an adequate level of protection.

When you use "Share", you choose whom to send the result card to. That card carries no ages or details about your family.

7. For how long

This policy is in force from November 25, 2026. We use the data only for what this policy says and for the time indicated in section 4. Ojito's database will remain in place while the service operates. When it stops operating, or when a piece of data is no longer needed and the law does not require keeping it, it is deleted.

8. Security

The connection to our servers and the loading of suggested options' photos are encrypted (HTTPS). Photos are in private storage. Only the server can change product scores. No system is completely secure.

9. Your rights

As the data subject, under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012, you can:

  • Know, update and correct your data.
  • Request proof of your authorization.
  • Know how it has been used.
  • Revoke the authorization or request that your data be deleted, unless there is a legal or contractual duty to keep it.
  • Access your data free of charge.
  • File complaints with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce, after first completing the inquiry or complaint process with us.

How to do it. Write to the email in section 1 saying what you are requesting and, if you can, the product's barcode. If someone requests it on behalf of another person or a minor, they must state in what capacity they act. Since there is no account, we may ask you for something that helps locate the information, because we don't know who sent each thing.

Deadlines. An inquiry is answered within at most 10 business days; if that is not possible, we tell you why and answer within the following 5 business days. A complaint is answered within at most 15 business days; if that is not possible, we tell you why and answer within the following 8 business days. If a complaint is incomplete, we tell you within the following 5 days so you can complete it; if 2 months pass without you completing it, it is considered withdrawn. Deadlines count from the day after we receive your request.

What a complaint must include. To correct, update or delete data, or to say that we did not comply with this policy or the law, the complaint must say who you are or on whose behalf you act, what happened, which email or address we should reply to and, if you have them, any documents you want to rely on. While we resolve it, we mark the data as "complaint in progress" (reclamo en trámite).

10. How to delete your data

  • On your phone: remove the profile in "My family" or uninstall the app.
  • Authorization for the text: withdraw it in Settings.
  • Identifier and Ojito Familia purchases: in Settings, "Delete my data from the server". Your subscription stays active in Google Play or the App Store; to use it again on that phone, tap "Restore purchases". To stop paying, cancel it in the store.
  • On our servers: write to the email in section 1 asking to delete your photos, your notes or your installation's identifier, with the product's barcode if you have it.

Product data already shown to everyone, such as its name or ingredients, does not say who sent it and may remain in the database. What the providers hold is deleted as described in section 6.

11. Children and adolescents

Ojito is for adults who check products, including for their children. A minor's age stays on the phone. Their rights are exercised by whoever represents them.

12. Product data

Product data comes in part from Open Food Facts and, in test versions, from Open Beauty Facts, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Product photos come from those same databases, under the CC BY-SA license. To complete the table and the list of a product, Ojito may read with artificial intelligence the images that Carulla, Éxito and Jumbo publish of that product on their websites. Those images are read and discarded: we only keep the data read and the address of the image as its source.

13. Health information

Ojito provides general information about packaged products. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. It does not replace your doctor, your pediatrician or a nutritionist. More detail in the terms and conditions.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this same address, with its number and date. If who is responsible for the data or what it is used for changes, we will tell you in the app before applying it, and if the purpose changes we will ask for a new authorization.
