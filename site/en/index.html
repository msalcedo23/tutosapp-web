OjitoOur productIn testing

Label scanner for families

Computer vision and nutrition rules

The problem
Reading a label at the supermarket takes time, and what works for an adult does not always work for a baby.
What we built
An app that reads the barcode and answers Good, Fair or Avoid for each person at home, with the reason, based on Colombian warning labels, additives and allergies.
The AI inside
  • Barcode reading with the camera
  • AI that reads the nutrition table and ingredients from a photo
  • Nutrition rules by age and allergy
Platforms

iOS and Android · Spanish and English

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

Resume RoastOur productAvailable

AI resume review

Document AI and scoring

The problem
Job seekers rarely know why their resume does not get past the filters, or what to change.
What we built
An app that reads a resume as text, PDF, Word or a photo, scores it out of 100, matches it to a job posting and rewrites it without inventing anything, with a PDF that applicant tracking systems can read.
The AI inside
  • Document AI: reads PDF, Word and photos
  • AI scoring and red flags
  • Guided rewrite for a job posting
Platforms

iOS and Android · Spanish and English

AI interview practice

A voice AI interviewer

The problem
Rehearsing with generic questions does not prepare you for the ones you will really be asked.
What we built
An AI interviewer that asks about your resume, adapts the pressure to your answers, listens to your voice and gives you a score and a verdict for the role.
The AI inside
  • Voice AI interviewer
  • Speech to text, without keeping the audio
  • A score and verdict calibrated to the role
Platforms

iOS and Android · Spanish and English

03How we work

AI agents do the heavy lifting. A person reviews every decision.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We get to know your idea, your users and the problem. We come out with a clear scope, what goes into the first version, and dates.

    People

  2. 02

    Design

    Screens and flows you can tap through on your phone before any code is written. AI explores options; you and we decide.

    AI + human review

  3. 03

    AI-built, human-reviewed

    AI agents write and test the code in parallel, and a person on the team reviews and approves every change before it goes in.

    AI + human review

  4. 04

    Quality

    Automated tests, accessibility review and testing on real phones, on iPhone and Android.

    AI + human review

  5. 05

    Launch on the App Store and Google Play

    Store listings, screenshots, privacy policy and terms, and getting through Apple’s and Google’s review.

    People

  6. 06

    Operation

    After launch: metrics, bugs, store updates and new features.

    AI + human review
