Resume Roast: support

Resume Roast is the AI that tells you what recruiters only think. Paste your CV, or upload a PDF, Word file or photo, and get an honest score out of 100, the red flags, concrete fixes and, if you add a job posting, how well you fit it. Then get your CV rewritten for that job and export it as a PDF.

Available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.

Contact

Write to hola@tutosapp.co. We usually answer within two business days, in English or Spanish.

It helps to include your app version: in the app, open About and tap the version line to copy it.

Frequently asked questions

How do credits work?

Your first 3 credits are free. One credit is one roast or one rewrite. If the AI fails, the credit comes back. Need more? Buy a pack once (5, 20 or 50 credits): no subscription, no ads and no account. Credits don't expire.

I bought credits but my balance didn't change.

Keep the app open with an internet connection for a few seconds, or close it and open it again: a purchase that was paid but not yet added is added on the next launch. If it still doesn't show, write to us with the date of the purchase and your app version.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases on iPhone are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund options.

Do I lose my credits if I delete the app?

On iPhone, credits belong to the device, not to an account. They stay if you delete and reinstall the app on the same iPhone. They don't move to a new phone or between iPhone and Android.

What happens to my CV?

There's no sign-up. Your CV text is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI provider, analyzed, and not saved on our servers. Your history, your photo and your PDFs stay on your device. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open History and clear it; deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone. To delete the credit balance kept for your device on our servers, write to us.

Which file types can I upload?

PDF, Word (.docx), and photos or scans of your CV (up to 6 files at once). You can also paste the text.

In which languages does it work?

The app is in English and Spanish. Your roast and your rewritten CV can be in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian or German.

The result looks wrong or offensive.

The feedback is written by AI and can make mistakes. Tell us at hola@tutosapp.co with what you saw, and we'll look into it.
