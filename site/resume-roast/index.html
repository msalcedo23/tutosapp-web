How much does it cost?

You start with 3 free credits. One credit is one roast or one rewrite, and if the AI fails you get the credit back. Need more? Buy a pack once: no subscription, no ads and no account.

Does the AI invent experience?

No. The rewrite reorganizes and sharpens what you already have for the job, but it never invents jobs, titles or experience.

What happens to my resume?

To analyze it, your resume text goes through our server and an AI provider, and it isn’t stored. Your history, photo and PDFs stay on your phone. The privacy policy has the details.

Which languages does it support?

The app is in English and Spanish, and your resume can come out in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian or German.

What is an ATS-friendly PDF?

Many companies use software that reads your resume before a person does. Resume Roast exports a PDF with selectable text that those systems can read, in three modern templates.

Is it on iPhone?

It’s on Google Play today. The iPhone version is coming soon: email us and we’ll let you know.
