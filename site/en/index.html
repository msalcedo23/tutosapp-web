How we work

Useful technology, no fine print.

  • AI that explains

    Every result comes with the reason: the label or ingredient in Ojito, the CV section in Resume Roast, the exact answer in Roast Interview.

  • Honest with you

    We tell you what we see, even when it is not what you want to hear. No brand pays to appear in Ojito, and Resume Roast never invents experience.

  • No sign-up to start

    Open the app and use it: none of them asks you to create an account. What costs money is said clearly, before you pay.

  • Made in Colombia

    We design in Medellín around how people here shop, look for work and talk, in Spanish and English.
