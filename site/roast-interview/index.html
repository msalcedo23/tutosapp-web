01Roast Interview

What it does for you

  • Questions from your resume

    Not a generic list: it spots inconsistencies and asks about them, like a real interviewer.

  • Adaptive pressure

    Strong answers raise the bar. If you stumble, it helps you get back on track.

  • A score for that job

    At the end you get your score, your detected level and what you did well, calibrated to the role.

  • Track your progress

    Retake the interview with new questions on the same core topics and compare your scores.

02How it works

Rehearse the interview before it counts.

  1. 01

    Load your resume

    Paste it or upload a PDF, Word file or photo. Add the job posting if you have it.

  2. 02

    Take the interview

    Answer by typing or out loud, with a clock per answer. The pressure adapts to how you are doing.

  3. 03

    Get your verdict

    Your score, detected level and what you did well. The full report adds red flags, your weakest moment with a better answer, and a plan to improve.

When does it launch?

We’re finishing testing. It’s coming soon to Google Play and the App Store: email us and we’ll tell you the day it launches.

How much will it cost?

Every day you get one free mini interview of 8 questions, with a score and a verdict. Full interviews with the full report are bought with credits, once, with no subscription.

Which language is the interview in?

Spanish or English. By default it follows the language of your resume, and you can change it before you start.

Can I answer out loud?

Yes. Answer by typing or with your voice; your voice is turned into text and the audio is not kept.

Do I need internet or an account?

You need internet during the interview. You don’t need to create an account.

What happens to my data?

Interview sessions are deleted after 24 hours, and you can delete your data from Settings. The privacy policy has the details.
